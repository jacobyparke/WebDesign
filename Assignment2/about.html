about Kobe Kolor

The purpose of Kobe Kolor is to serve as a public figure or client's landing page for their social media, projects, and creative work.

The site functions as an alternative to a traditional Linktree-style page while creating a more personalized visual experience.

The goal is to clearly display the client's social media channels and creative work while using imagery to communicate their visual identity.

A rotating mood board provides additional inspiration and connects the client's interests and influences to their work.

Because too many links and visual choices can overwhelm visitors, the site is intentionally organized to keep the experience easy to follow.

Clear links, readable typography, consistent visual design, and relevant imagery help users navigate the site without unnecessary distractions.
